Privacy policy

Effective 25 September 2026

Books Reader is a private tool used by one person, Adam Isaacson, for his own accounting work. It has no other users.

What it reads

Financial reports from QuickBooks Online companies that Adam has been given access to as their accountant, such as the profit and loss statement, balance sheet and receivables ageing. It reads only. It never writes to QuickBooks.

Where the data goes

Report data is saved only on Adam's own computer and his own private storage, kept separately for each company. It is never sold, never shared with anyone else, and never used for advertising.

AI assistant

Adam uses an AI assistant, Claude by Anthropic, to read and analyse the downloaded reports on his behalf. The assistant sees report data only while doing that work. It is never given the QuickBooks sign-in tokens or the app's secret keys.

Sign-in tokens

The access tokens QuickBooks issues are stored encrypted on Adam's computer. They are never published and never sent to any other service.

Disconnecting

A company can be disconnected at any time from within QuickBooks Online. After that, the tool can read nothing further from it.

This website

These pages collect no personal data and set no cookies.
